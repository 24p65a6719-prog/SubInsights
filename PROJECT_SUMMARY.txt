🎯 WHAT PROBLEMS DOES IT SOLVE?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE PROBLEM:
  Users have $3,000-$10,000 in unused annual benefits from:
  • Professional memberships (IEEE, IEI)
  • Premium credit cards (AmEx Platinum)
  • Hotel loyalty programs (Hilton Honors)
  
  ...but DON'T USE THEM because:
  ✗ Don't know benefits exist
  ✗ Don't know where to claim them
  ✗ Don't know if they're still valid
  ✗ Benefits seem too complicated

THE SOLUTION:
  SubInsights combines three technologies:
  1. GEOFENCING: Detects when you're near a participating merchant
  2. BERT VERIFICATION: Confirms benefits are actively being honored
  3. SMART ALERTS: Sends you a notification with redemption code
  
  RESULT: Users reclaim $2,000-$5,000/year in hidden value!
